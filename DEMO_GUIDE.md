## Judge answers
How does your AI work? "It combines transparent rules for urgency, credential requests, fake rewards and suspicious links with an optional machine-learning model, then explains every point of the score."
How accurate is it? "It is an educational risk assessment, not a guarantee. I measured it on test messages and it can make mistakes, so it always recommends verifying independently."
Different from ChatGPT? "It works offline, shows exactly which signs raised the score, saves incident records and teaches users through a challenge."
Does it report criminals automatically? "No. It prepares a report and the user decides whether to open the official channel."
